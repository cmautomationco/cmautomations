## 3. The Solution (brief: 0:50–2:20)

**0:52–1:02** · **on camera**  
So I build the system in between. From the moment someone pays to their first check-in, every step is connected, and you don’t have to hire anyone.

**1:03–1:08** · screen recording: Send a test sign-up to a demo coaching business account.  
Here’s a new client signing up on a demo coaching account. That’s point A.

**1:09–1:17** · screen recording: Show the instant reply arriving.  
Within seconds, they’ve got a welcome message, their intake form, and a link to book their kickoff call. That’s point B.

**1:18–1:30** · screen recording: Show the follow-up nudge.  
If the form doesn’t come back, they get a friendly nudge after thirty minutes, another at midday, and a final one in the 5pm sweep. Nothing goes out after seven on weekdays.

**1:32–1:41** · screen recording: Show the enquiry landing in the spreadsheet.  
Every client’s onboarding sits in one sheet. Form back, call booked, programme sent. You can see who’s stuck at a glance. That’s point C.

**1:42–1:49** · screen recording: Show the weekly report.  
And every week, one report shows who’s started, who’s checking in, and who needs a word from you. That’s point D.

**1:50–1:58**  
It can also chase missed check-ins, nudge clients to rebook, and send reminders before calls. That’s all in the breakdown.

**1:58–2:15**  
There’s no one new to hire. The system runs every day, and we keep an eye on it for you. If anything breaks, needs changing, or could work better, we’re alerted and we let you know. You coach. The system handles the onboarding. You check one report on a Friday.
